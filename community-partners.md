The SLV Foundation for Education believes that strong schools are built by strong communities. We are grateful for the organizations, businesses, and individuals who partner with us to support public education in San Lorenzo Valley.

## Become a Partner

We welcome partnerships with local businesses, organizations, and community members who share our commitment to public education.

**Partner with us to:**
- Sponsor scholarships or specific programs
- Provide in-kind support to schools
- Connect students with internships and mentorship opportunities
- Co-sponsor community events that benefit SLV schools
