The SLV Foundation for Education is proud to support students pursuing higher education through our scholarship program.

## About Our Scholarships

We award scholarships to graduating seniors from San Lorenzo Valley High School and other students within the San Lorenzo Valley Unified School District who demonstrate academic achievement, community involvement, and a commitment to their future.
