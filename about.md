## Who We Are

The SLV Foundation for Education is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization dedicated to funding and supporting public education in the San Lorenzo Valley Unified School District.

Founded in 2003, we have worked to connect the broader SLV community with the schools that serve our children — bridging gaps, responding to emerging needs, and building lasting partnerships that strengthen education throughout the valley.

## Our Mission

To help the students of the San Lorenzo Valley Unified School District achieve success in an increasingly complex, diverse, and challenging world.

## Our Vision

Excellence in public education — for all students.

## What We Do

- **Engage the community** in meaningful support for public schools
- **Respond to emerging educational needs** across the district
- **Foster collaborative projects** with community partners
- **Award scholarships** to help SLV students pursue higher education
- **Support programs** that expand opportunities for all students
