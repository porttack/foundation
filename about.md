The SLV Foundation for Education (SLVFE) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization officially formed and endorsed by the state of California on November 1, 2002. We work with the community to raise funds and support school initiatives throughout the San Lorenzo Valley Unified School District.

## Annual Meeting

Our annual meeting is held on the **Tuesday before May 15th** each year at **5:00pm** in the SLVUSD District Office Board Room, 325 Marion Ave, Ben Lomond. All community members are welcome to attend.
